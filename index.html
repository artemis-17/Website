01 / CURRENT WORK

Research focus.

I study how several small spacecraft can divide the work of inspecting a rotating object. My work combines relative orbital motion, controlled hovering, and mission planning, with attention to fuel use, observation coverage, collision avoidance, and communication.

I’m a PhD researcher in the Autonomous Space Vehicles and Astrodynamics Lab at the University of Arizona, advised by Dr. Eric Butcher. I’m also interested in cislunar trajectory design.

More about my background ↗

02 / SELECTED PROJECT

Master’s thesis.

I developed a genetic algorithm to plan an inspection mission around a tumbling spacecraft. The method assigns spacecraft to natural relative orbits or controlled hovering tasks, then compares candidate plans for fuel use, coverage, collision avoidance, and network connectivity.
